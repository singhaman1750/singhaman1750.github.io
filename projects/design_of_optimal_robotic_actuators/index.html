Design of Optimal Robotic Actuators

Computational design of planetary-gearbox actuators for legged robots

← Back to research themes

The actuator sets the ceiling on what a legged robot can do. Gear ratio, stage topology, and motor choice together fix torque density, reflected inertia, and thermal headroom — yet these are typically picked by hand from catalogue parts. This theme treats the actuator as a design variable to be optimized numerically, and builds the tooling to fabricate and test the result.
