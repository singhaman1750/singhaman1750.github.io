Robot Platforms and Locomotion

Building quadrupeds and the control that makes them robust in hardware

← Back to research themes

Design and co-design claims only mean something once a robot survives contact with the floor. This theme covers the platforms built to test them — quadrupeds designed for serviceability and repeatable experiments — together with the locomotion controllers that run on them. The recurring question is which modelling and control choices still hold up once real actuators, real compliance, and real impacts are in the loop.
